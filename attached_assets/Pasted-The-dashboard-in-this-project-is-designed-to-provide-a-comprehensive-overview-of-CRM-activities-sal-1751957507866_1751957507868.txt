The dashboard in this project is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of CRM activities, sales performance, and AI-powered tools. It is structured into several distinct sections, which can be reordered and toggled for visibility through the Dashboard Layout Controls.

Here's a breakdown of the current dashboard design, including its sections and their key components:

Dashboard Sections and Components
The dashboard is composed of the following sections, presented in their default order:

Executive Overview Section

Description: Provides a high-level summary of key performance indicators (KPIs) and quick actions for immediate tasks.
Components:
DashboardHeader: Displays a welcoming message, the current date, and a summary of top-level metrics such as Monthly Revenue, Growth Rate, and New Customers.
KPICards: Showcases critical sales metrics like "Active Deals," "Pipeline Value," "Won Deals," and "Avg Deal Size." Each card includes a value, a trend indicator, and relevant contact avatars for visual context.
QuickActions: Offers a set of prominent buttons for frequently performed actions, such as "New Deal," "Add Contact," "Schedule Meeting," and "Send Email," often displaying dynamic counts or associated contact avatars.
MetricsCards: (Optional/Secondary) Provides additional key performance metrics, similar to KPICards but potentially with different data points or presentation.
AI Smart Features Hub

Description: Centralizes AI-powered insights and productivity tools, leveraging various AI models for enhanced CRM operations.
Components:
AIInsightsPanel: Presents real-time AI-generated insights into pipeline health, potential deal risks, and conversion opportunities. It includes a "Generate Insights" button and displays related contact avatars.
SmartAIControls: Offers a control panel for executing AI analysis on contacts, including smart scoring, enrichment, categorization, and lead qualification. It allows setting urgency levels and provides model recommendations.
AIModelUsageStats: Displays detailed statistics on AI model consumption, showing total cost, API requests, tokens used, and average response times, broken down by individual models.
LiveDealAnalysis: Provides real-time analysis of active deals, highlighting high-value opportunities, stalled deals, and hot prospects, with associated contact avatars.
SmartSearchRealtime: Implements a real-time search interface that allows users to semantically search across contacts, deals, and companies, with AI-enhanced results.
Sales Pipeline & Analytics

Description: Offers comprehensive insights into the sales pipeline, deal performance, and conversion metrics.
Components:
DealAnalytics: Presents a detailed view of deal performance, including KPI metrics (Total Revenue, Conversion Rate, Total Contacts, Avg Deal Size), summary cards for active, won, and lost deals, trend charts (Revenue Performance, Pipeline Value by Month), pipeline distribution charts, and stage performance breakdowns.
ChartsSection: Visualizes key sales analytics through various charts, such as performance trends (revenue, deals closed), pipeline stage distribution, and breakdown by product/service categories.
Conversion Metrics: A dedicated section that highlights conversion rates between different stages of the sales funnel (e.g., Lead to Opportunity, Opportunity to Proposal, Proposal to Win).
Customer & Lead Management

Description: Focuses on managing customer relationships and nurturing leads through various stages.
Components:
NewLeadsSection: Displays a dedicated area for newly acquired leads, showcasing individual contact cards for "hot" leads.
CustomerProfile: Provides a detailed view of a selected customer's profile, including their contact information, company details, social media profiles, and custom fields.
Activities & Communications

Description: Helps users manage their daily tasks, track communications, and oversee appointments.
Components:
TasksSection: Lists daily tasks, including highlighted priority tasks and those assigned to specific team members.
InteractionHistory: Shows a chronological record of interactions (e.g., deals, calls, emails) with contacts, categorized by their status.
AppointmentWidget: Displays a list of upcoming appointments, detailing their type, scheduled time, and description.
Quick Communications: Provides quick access to communication-related items, such as email follow-ups and meeting notes.
TasksAndFunnel: Combines a calendar-based tasks schedule (showing tasks assigned to team members) with a visual representation of the sales stage funnel.
RecentActivity: Presents a feed of recent activities within the CRM and highlights upcoming deals, including status updates and associated contact information.
Integrations & System

Description: Manages connected applications and provides access to core system settings.
Components:
ConnectedApps: Lists all integrated applications, providing links and brief descriptions, often categorized by the team or function they serve.
System Settings: Allows configuration of default AI models and displays the connection status of various external APIs (e.g., Google AI, OpenAI, Supabase), indicating whether they are connected or require setup.
Each component is styled using Tailwind CSS utility classes directly within its JSX, ensuring a consistent and responsive design across the application.
